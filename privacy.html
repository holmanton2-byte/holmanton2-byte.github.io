Fyr – Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Fyr is a personal assistant run by its single owner on the owner's own computer.

  • What it accesses: the owner's Google Calendar, via the Google Calendar API, to read events and add events the owner approves.
  • Where data goes: calendar data is processed on the owner's computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Who uses it: only the owner. No other users' data is collected.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Fyr's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
